## Purpose and audience

This cluster serves parents, teachers, and beginner learners researching how Quranic Arabic reading develops from letter recognition to guided word reading. The interactive platform is positioned for ages 3–12. Live NoorPath classes remain a separate service for ages 4+ and are owned by `/courses/noorani-qaida-online`.

## Competitor gaps

Observed results are dominated by app listings, PDF download pages, and live-class sales pages. The strongest defensible gaps are:

1. A transparent 11-module learning map rather than an unexplained “complete course” claim.
2. One connected journey from letter recognition to Harakaat, joining, word reading, revision, and guided Quranic practice.
3. Age-aware parent and teacher guidance beside learner activities.
4. Clear descriptions of what each game practises, rather than games presented without educational outcomes.
5. Honest separation between self-practice, audio guidance, and teacher-verified pronunciation.
6. Progress, rewards, and revision presented as motivation tools rather than guarantees of mastery.
7. Accessible English explanations with Arabic examples and right-to-left presentation.

## Publishing and claim constraints

- Curriculum explanations are educational support, not an Ijazah, Tajweed certificate, or substitute for qualified correction.
- Audio and pronunciation claims must describe only shipped functionality. “Qari-reviewed” remains prohibited until reviewed files are recorded, approved, and linked in the product manifest.
- Completion is learner-paced. Do not promise a fixed number of weeks or months.
- Keep 28 letters as the site-wide convention unless editorial review formally adopts a separate Hamza treatment.
- Screenshots must be captioned as a NoorPath learning-platform preview and must not imply public demo access.
- FAQPage and Course schema can support machine understanding, but copy must not promise retired Google rich-result treatments.
