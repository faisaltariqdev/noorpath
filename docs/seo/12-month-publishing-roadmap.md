# Twelve-Month Nine-Country Publishing Roadmap

Status: operational roadmap; no article copy is included  
Current review date: 15 July 2026

This roadmap sequences page refreshes, evidence-gated publishing, outreach and measurement. It does not author articles or approve a page solely because it appears in a future month. The ownership register remains the source of truth for canonical URLs and release gates.

## Recurring operating cadence

Every week:

- Check indexability, canonical, redirect, sitemap, structured-data and hreflang faults.
- Check for new links to redirect sources and new query-to-page ownership conflicts.
- Record factual corrections and time-sensitive service changes.

Every 28 days:

- Compare non-brand impressions, clicks, click-through rate, indexed canonical pages, qualified trial requests, completed trials and relevant referring domains by country and owner.
- Review whether internal links route queries to the registered canonical owner.
- Decide whether a refresh, consolidation or no change is supported by evidence.

Before every release:

- Confirm one owner per intent.
- Confirm factual and human editorial review.
- Confirm no unsupported metrics, credentials, ratings, outcomes or physical-presence claims.
- Confirm required parent/service/trust links.
- Confirm that the work is assigned to the correct country allocation.

## Non-duplication rule

The roadmap permits universal guides, country hubs and evidence-gated city utility. It does not permit nine country clones of one article, country-specific copies of global service pages, or city pages made by token replacement. If regional value fits in the country hub or a concise section of a global owner, update that owner instead of creating another URL.
